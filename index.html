Terms, Privacy & Guidelines

Last updated: October 2026. Plain-language rules for using LoreCraft Studio.

Terms of Use Privacy Policy Community Guidelines

Terms of Use

By creating an account or publishing on LoreCraft Studio ("LoreCraft", "we"), you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use the site.

1. Who can use LoreCraft

You must be at least 13 years old to create an account. Stories rated Mature 18+ are only for readers who are 18 or older. If you are under 18, please use LoreCraft with a parent's or guardian's permission.

2. Your account

Keep your password private. You're responsible for what's posted from your account. You can delete your account at any time from Account > Delete my account.

3. Your stories belong to you

You keep the rights to everything you write. When you publish, you allow LoreCraft to store, display and share your work on the site (for example in the Public Library, on your author page, and in shared links) for as long as it stays published. You can unpublish or delete your work at any time. Only publish work that is yours or that you have permission to share.

4. Content rules

Everything you post (stories, comments, reviews, broadcasts and profile details) must follow the Community Guidelines. Mark mature content with the right rating and content warnings.

5. Moderation

Readers can report stories, comments and reviews. Moderators may hide stories or remove content that breaks these rules, and may suspend accounts that repeatedly do.

6. The service

LoreCraft is provided as it is, without guarantees. Features may change, and the site may sometimes be unavailable. Keep your own backups (use Backup or Export). We're not responsible for content posted by other users.

7. Changes

We may update these terms. If the changes are important, we'll show a notice on the site. Continuing to use LoreCraft after a change means you accept the updated terms.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (handled by Google Firebase; we never see your password).
  • Profile: pen name, handle, bio, location, website and avatar, if you add them.
  • What you create: drafts, chapters, lore, audio links, series, broadcasts, comments, reviews, votes and follows.
  • Reading activity: your bookmarks, reading progress and display settings, saved to your account so they follow you between devices.
  • Read counts: when you open a chapter, its read count goes up by one. This count isn't linked to your account.

What is public

Published stories, your author page (pen name, bio, avatar, location, website and latest broadcasts), your comments and reviews (with your pen name), and who you follow. Your email address, drafts, unpublished work, lore you haven't added to a character guide, bookmarks and reading progress are private.

Services we use

LoreCraft runs on GitHub Pages (website hosting) and Google Firebase (accounts and database). Google reCAPTCHA / Fraud Defense checks that visitors are real people, to protect the site from bots. These services may process technical data such as your IP address and browser details under their own privacy policies. Fonts and icons load from Google Fonts and cdnjs. We don't sell your data or show ads.

Stored on your device

LoreCraft saves your work and settings in your browser's local storage, and keeps a copy of stories you've opened so they work offline. Clearing your browser data removes these copies, but your account copies stay safe if you're signed in.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from Account > Delete my account. This permanently removes your account, your published stories (with their comments, reviews and votes), your comments, reviews, votes and follows on other stories, your author page and your saved drafts. Reports you've sent may be kept so moderators can finish reviewing them.

Children

LoreCraft isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, please report it so it can be removed.

Questions

For privacy questions or requests, contact the site owner.

Community Guidelines

LoreCraft is a place for storytellers and readers. Dark and difficult themes are welcome in fiction when they're clearly rated and marked with content warnings. These are not allowed:

  • Any sexual content involving minors, in any form.
  • Harassment, bullying, threats or hate aimed at real people or groups.
  • Content that encourages or gives instructions for self-harm, suicide or real-world violence.
  • Sharing someone's private information without permission.
  • Plagiarism: posting someone else's writing or art as your own, or without permission.
  • Spam, scams, advertising, fake accounts, or manipulating votes, reads or reviews.
  • Mature content without the Mature 18+ rating and content warnings.

Be kind in comments and reviews: critique the story, not the person. If you see something that breaks these rules, use the Report button. Moderators review every report.
